\section{Review Limitations}

Five limitations bound the reading of the results and qualify the reach of the conclusions.

\noindent\textbf{Granularity of the first-pass extraction.} The thematic synthesis was derived mainly from the structured fields of the records (artifacts, roles, execution, validation, portability, risks, nature, and evidence), together with about sixteen substantive inclusion justifications; the remaining records carry a more generic inclusion justification. Themes and findings were therefore complemented by the known public identity of well-documented frameworks, such as ChatDev, MetaGPT, AutoCodeRover, Reversa, and AgileGen. A second extraction pass with a narrative findings field per study would deepen the synthesis.

\noindent\textbf{No quality weighting.} The protocol fixed \texttt{quality\_assessment: none}. As a result, findings from arXiv preprints not yet peer-reviewed (ten studies) and from studies with medium extraction confidence (two) enter with the same nominal weight as peer-reviewed work. The strength of the evidence is qualified by source category in the synthesis, but there is no formal exclusion or weighting by risk of bias.

\noindent\textbf{Language and database scope.} The protocol restricts the review to English and Portuguese, in the 2018 to 2026 window. The direct arXiv search failed in part due to access instability, with preprint coverage recovered via Semantic Scholar and the arXiv DOI, which may have left out very recent preprints not indexed by those routes. A publication bias toward frameworks with positive results is likely, given that studies of failure or non-adoption are rare in the corpus.

\noindent\textbf{Operational pending item outside the corpus.} One source approved at screening lacked full text obtained at the cutoff date and was kept as an operational pending item, outside the extraction and this synthesis. The corpus effectively synthesized is 37 studies; incorporating that source later would adjust marginal counts without changing the thematic structure.

\noindent\textbf{Snowballing limit.} Snowballing was stopped at the protocol limit of three rounds. The last rounds still added included studies, three in the second and three in the third, which suggests the corpus may not have reached full saturation; additional rounds could surface relevant studies, especially outside the communities of origin of the seeds.
