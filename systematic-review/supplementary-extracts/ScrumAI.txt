# ScrumAI
DOI: 10.1109/ubmk67458.2025.11206875
source: s2

In the software development process, it is crucial to minimize planning time and accelerate operational activities. Especially in agile methodologies such as Scrum, sprint planning meetings can consume a significant amount of time, and conducting daily stand-up meetings face-to-face may not be efficient for some teams. The purpose of this project is to alleviate the workload of teams who use Scrum by providing LLM-based solutions. The system offers the following functionalities: generating a requirement list, creating a product backlog, building sprint backlogs, and managing daily stand-up meetings. To implement these functionalities, Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Google-Gemma-2-9b-it models were fine-tuned. The Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct model was responsible for creating the requirement lists, while the Google-Gemma-2-9b-it model handled the generation of product and sprint backlogs, as well as the management of daily stand-up meetings. Therefore, the Llama model is referred to as the "Requirement LLM" and the Gemma model is referred to as the "Scrum LLM" throughout the project. In the project, the requirements were stored in a relational database, while the backlog information was stored in the Jira application. Function calling techniques were employed to enable the Scrum LLM to interact with the Jira API. This allowed the Scrum model to interpret and execute backend functions, which we write. Our results show that the outputs generated by the Scrum and Requirement LLMs were structurally similar to the reference texts and provided semantically coherent responses.
