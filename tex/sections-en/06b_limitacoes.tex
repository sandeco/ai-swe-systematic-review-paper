\section{Review Limitations}

Five limitations bound the reading of the results and qualify the reach of the conclusions.

\noindent\textbf{Self-reported and heterogeneous results.} The thematic synthesis derives from the structured fields of the records (artifacts, roles, execution, validation, portability, risks, nature, and evidence) and from a narrative finding per study. For the twenty-five protocol studies whose first-pass justification was generic, a second pass over the full text recovered the central finding and the self-reported results; the four studies added by the supplementary search were extracted at the abstract level, since no open-access full text was available. The caveat that remains is that these results are self-reported by the authors in heterogeneous setups, with different models, tasks, and metrics and no shared benchmark to compare them directly; they are therefore read as evidence for each proposal rather than as a comparable measure across frameworks, and the absence of such a benchmark is treated as a gap in the Research Agenda.

\noindent\textbf{Quality assessment by category, not by full text.} We applied a four-item source-category quality assessment (Table~\ref{tab:qualidade}) that weights the strength of the evidence and flags the lower-confidence studies, but it does not replace a formal risk-of-bias instrument applied to the full text of each study. The ten arXiv preprints not yet peer-reviewed and the six studies with medium extraction confidence, which include the four extracted at the abstract level by the supplementary search, enter the synthesis with a cautious reading, and no theme rests exclusively on them; even so, a full-text reappraisal with a validated instrument would refine the weighting.

\noindent\textbf{Language and database scope, and publication bias.} The protocol restricts the review to English and Portuguese, in the 2018 to 2026 window. The direct arXiv search failed in part due to access instability, with preprint coverage recovered via Semantic Scholar and the arXiv DOI, which may have left out very recent preprints not indexed by those routes. A publication bias toward frameworks with positive results is likely and measurable in the corpus itself: 28 of the 41 studies (68\%) claim empirical evaluation, almost always favorable to the proposed framework, while studies of failure, abandonment, or non-adoption are practically absent; only the adoption axis of theme T5, with six studies, problematizes organizational integration. Widening the search to additional databases and lifting the language restriction is future work that could balance this asymmetry.

\noindent\textbf{Operational pending items outside the corpus.} Two sources approved at screening lacked full text or an abstract at the cutoff date (one from the original protocol and one from the supplementary search) and were kept as operational pending items, outside the extraction and this synthesis. The corpus effectively synthesized is 41 studies; incorporating those sources later would adjust marginal counts without changing the thematic structure.

\noindent\textbf{Partial saturation and search recall.} Snowballing was stopped at the protocol limit of three rounds. The saturation curve (Figure~\ref{fig:saturacao}) shows decreasing inclusions, from 25 in the initial search to 6, 3, and 3 across the three rounds: a clear but nonzero deceleration. To audit completeness, a supplementary search on additional access routes (the direct arXiv API and Crossref), run with the same descriptors, recovered twelve in-scope studies not retrieved by the original search: five within the protocol window (2025 or earlier) and seven at or after the May 2026 cutoff. This confirms that the original search did not reach full recall in a fast-growing field. Four of the five within-window studies, all peer-reviewed, were incorporated into the corpus by this update, which grew from 37 to 41; the fifth was kept as an operational pending item for lack of a recoverable abstract. The seven post-cutoff studies fall outside the review window by construction and remain documented for a future update.
